Antoine Ratouchniak

CV

Hello! I am Antoine, an Applied Scientist at Amazon working on machine learning for job recommendations. I graduated with a master's degree in mathematics and artificial intelligence (MVA) from ENS Paris-Saclay.

I previously completed a research internship in the Brain & AI team at Meta FAIR, under the supervision of Hubert J. Banville and Jean-Rémi King. Before that, I was a visiting researcher at NYU Video Lab, supervised by Yao Wang and Adeen Flinker.

My research interests include representation learning, self-supervised learning, multimodal models, and applications of machine learning to neuroscience.
